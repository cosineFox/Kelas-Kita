# Urgent-removal runbook

Status: **not signed**. Keep public submissions closed until real people complete and record the drill below.

## Coverage contract

- Primary operator: value recorded in `URGENT_REMOVAL_PRIMARY`.
- Backup operator: value recorded in `URGENT_REMOVAL_BACKUP`.
- Public route: value recorded in `OPERATOR_CONTACT_EMAIL` and the final privacy notice.
- Urgent target: acknowledge a credible threat or exposed personal information within 30 minutes during published coverage hours.
- Safety target: temporarily hold first, preserve the private audit record, then investigate. Do not wait for a truth or defamation judgement before hiding exposed contact details or a credible threat.
- If 30-minute coverage cannot be staffed, submissions remain closed outside staffed hours.

## Live response

1. Open `/moderation`, confirm the case ID and urgent age, and take ownership through the agreed operator channel.
2. Check the content, report context, Qwen categories and deterministic Core action. Treat model prose as a signal, not evidence.
3. For an exposed phone number, address, identity number or credible targeted threat, select **Hold** or **Remove** and write a specific private policy reason.
4. Preserve only the minimum record needed for appeal and incident handling. Do not copy the content into chat, tickets or personal notes.
5. Notify the backup if the primary cannot finish within 15 minutes.
6. For imminent danger, follow the operator’s counsel-approved emergency escalation. KelasKita is not an emergency service or investigative body.
7. Send the affected person the appeal/right-of-reply route when contact is available and appropriate.
8. Record acknowledgement, action and closure timestamps. Review false positives after the urgent risk is contained.

## Required staging drill

Use synthetic data only—never a real lecturer’s contact details or a real allegation.

| Check | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Primary receives alert | Timestamp and channel |
| Primary acknowledges | Under 15 minutes |
| Synthetic review leaves public feed | Under 30 minutes |
| Private decision contains a reason | Case ID and timestamp |
| Backup takes over a second case | Handover timestamps |
| Appeal restores a safe redacted item | Appeal and decision IDs |
| AI Gateway unavailable | Item remains pending; retry is visible and works |
| Operator session revoked | Old cookie returns 401 after expiry/logout |
